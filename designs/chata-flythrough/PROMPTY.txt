FLYTHROUGH — prompty pro image-to-video (5 klipů dle PORADI.txt)
Každý klip = jeden nepřerušený pohyb kamery, ~5-8 s.
Startovní snímek = odpovídající foto. Vždy image-to-video, ne text-to-video.

===========================================================
KLIP 1 — 01_exterier_den.jpg — otevírací záběr (den)
===========================================================
Aerial drone footage of a handcrafted log cabin, beginning high and wide with an
establishing view of the full property, the manicured lawn, the stacked stone
retaining wall and the surrounding evergreen forest, then smoothly and
continuously descending and pushing forward toward the covered wooden deck. The
drone moves in a single uninterrupted arc — no cuts, no edits — gradually
lowering altitude and orbiting slightly as it settles on the deck, its timber
railing and the tall triangular gable windows. Preserve the exact architecture,
round log walls, roof pitch, window layout, deck furniture placement, landscaping
and stone wall from the reference image. The movement feels like a professional
cinematic drone operator — one confident, fluid pass, no cuts, no teleporting, no
jump edits. Maintain realistic depth, natural parallax and consistent scale
throughout. Bright sun-drenched early-autumn atmosphere, photorealistic quality.
End the shot hovering just above lawn level with a clear sweeping view of the
deck and cabin facade.

===========================================================
KLIP 2 — 02_terasa.jpg — přechod ven → dovnitř
===========================================================
Slow cinematic forward glide across a wooden cabin deck at eye level, drifting
gently past two white Adirondack chairs toward the timber railing and the open
valley view beyond. The camera moves in one continuous unbroken push, with a
subtle rise at the end. Preserve the exact deck planking, railing design, chair
placement, round log wall on the left and the autumn treeline in the background
from the reference image. Soft warm late-afternoon light, natural haze in the
distance, realistic parallax between railing posts and background trees.
Photorealistic, no cuts, no camera shake, no people.

Slow cinematic interior dolly through a warmly lit log cabin kitchen and dining

KLIP 5 — 05_exterier_soumrak.jpg — návrat ven + day→dusk (a loop)
===========================================================
Aerial drone footage of the same handcrafted log cabin at blue hour, the camera
slowly rising and pulling back from the glowing deck to a wide establishing view
of the property, the lawn and the stone retaining wall, in one continuous
unbroken move. Warm amber interior light pours through the tall gable windows
and the porch sconces; the deep blue twilight sky and dark evergreen silhouettes
stay stable throughout. Preserve the exact architecture, window layout, deck
railing, landscaping and stone wall from the reference image. Subtle natural
light bloom from the windows, no flicker, no cuts, no camera shake, no people.
Photorealistic luxury real estate drone tour aesthetic.

--- varianta pro time-lapse den→soumrak (01 → 05 jako jeden klip) ---
Použij 01_exterier_den.jpg jako první snímek a 05_exterier_soumrak.jpg jako
poslední snímek (first/last frame mode). Prompt:
Static-to-slow-drift exterior shot of a log cabin, a smooth continuous time-lapse
transition from bright sunny afternoon to deep blue twilight: the sunlight fades,
shadows lengthen, the sky deepens to blue, and the interior lights and porch
sconces gradually glow on. The camera holds nearly still with only a very slow
subtle push in. Architecture, landscaping and stone wall remain perfectly stable
and identical throughout. Photorealistic, no cuts, no flicker, no morphing
geometry, no people.

NEGATIVE PROMPT (do všech klipů)
===========================================================
people, humans, text, watermark, logo, camera shake, jump cut, morphing
architecture, warping walls, extra windows, extra buildings, swimming pool,
distortion, fisheye, motion blur artifacts, oversaturated colors, lens flare
artifacts, duplicated furniture, flickering lights

===========================================================
POZNÁMKY
===========================================================
- Objekt NEMÁ bazén. Nikdy nepiš "pool", "pool area" ani "outdoor lounge" —
  model by ho dogeneroval.
- Kratší klipy = menší drift. 5-6 s drží geometrii líp než 10 s.
- Když se stavba začne "vlnit", zpomal pohyb kamery (méně "descending and
  pushing forward", víc "slow subtle drift").
- 01 a 05 = stejný vantage point → konec klipu 5 navazuje zpět na začátek
  klipu 1 pro seamless loop.
